# ISO 27001:2022 Annex A Control Mapping

This mapping links the Microsoft 365 hardening lab activities to relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls. It is intended to support lab evidence collection, control traceability, and audit-readiness discussions. It is not a substitute for a formal Statement of Applicability or risk assessment.

## Usage guidance

Use this table as a starting point when preparing evidence packs or audit walkthroughs. Adjust the mapping to match your tenant scope, selected licence tier, risk assessment, and Statement of Applicability decisions.
